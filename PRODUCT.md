# Product: JBFE Sea to Sea website

- **What:** Marketing and info site for JBFE Sea to Sea South (7–11 May 2027), a 3-day navigational team enduro through the tribal lands of the Transkei on the Wild Coast, hotel to hotel (Trennerys Hotel (Seagulls) and Kob Inn). Run by Red Cherry Events, title sponsor JBFE.
- **Who:** Mostly men 35–64 on enduro bikes, entering as teams of mates, often with their kids. Many are repeat riders. Read on phones in the paddock or hotel bar.
- **Job of the site:** Get teams to enter (primary CTA: Enter your team), then answer ride-info questions so the office gets fewer calls.
- **Voice:** A mate who has ridden every metre. South African, warm, cheeky, never macho. It's not a race.
- **Design system:** Sea to Sea design system (claude.ai artifact). Night theme by default; Sun for one CTA per view; Sky secondary; grade colours only for Gold/Silver/Adventure.
- **Mode:** Persuade (home, entries); Read (ride info, route).
